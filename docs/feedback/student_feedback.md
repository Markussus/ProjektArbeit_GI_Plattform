# Student Usability & Testing Feedback

**Project:** GI Learning Platform (Grundlagen der Informatik / Theoretical Computer Science)  
**Target Group:** Computer Science Students (THWS)  
**Date:** Summer Semester 2026  
**Status:** Evaluation & Testing Phase  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A usability and testing evaluation was conducted with students to assess the user experience, visual clarity, interactive simulations, and overall navigation of the **GI Learning Platform**. 

The overall response was exceptionally positive:
- **Smooth and responsive performance:** Interactive simulations (Turing Machine, Finite Automata, DNF, Moore/Mealy machines, Number Systems and Encoding) ran smoothly with immediate state transitions and zero perceived latency.
- **High discoverability:** Topics, interactive modules, and exercises were easy to find without cognitive overhead.
- **Polished visuals:** Clean modern design, effective color coding, and high-quality diagram rendering (state graphs, logic gates, truth tables) significantly aided comprehension.
- **Intuitive navigation:** Seamless switching between module overviews, theory concepts, interactive simulators, and exercise validation panels.

---

## 2. Key Feedback Themes

### 2.1. Smooth Performance & Interactive Simulators
Students highlighted the fluid and snappy feel of the platform:
- Step-by-step execution and interactive controls (play, pause, speed adjustment, single-step) respond instantly.
- Turing Machine tape animations and State Machine transitions update seamlessly without jitter.
- Reset controls and parameter modifications apply immediately, making experimentation quick and engaging.

### 2.2. Clear Structure & "Everything Easy to Find"
The information architecture received praise for its simplicity and logical grouping:
- The top-level separation between modules (e.g., *Fundamentals of Computer Science*, *Programming 1*) is straightforward.
- Within each topic, the dedicated tabs for **Theory / Overview**, **Interactive Simulation**, and **Exercises** provide a consistent learning path.
- Clear search and filter options allow students to jump directly to specific topics (e.g., *DNF / Boolean Algebra*, *Finite Automata*, *Moore & Mealy*, *Number Systems*, *Turing Machine*, *Encoding*).

### 2.3. Outstanding Visuals & Diagram Quality
The visual design was cited as one of the platform's greatest strengths:
- Diagrams (State Machine graphs, logic gates, binary representations) are visually clean and easy to interpret.
- Proper use of typography, spacing, and contrast reduces eye strain during extended study sessions.
- Full support for both light and dark modes with balanced color palettes.

### 2.4. Intuitive Navigation
- The persistent sidebar and top navigation allow quick switching between different topics without losing context.
- Header transfer boxes and action buttons are positioned where users intuitively expect them.
- Clear breadcrumbs and page intros keep users oriented across multi-step learning modules.
